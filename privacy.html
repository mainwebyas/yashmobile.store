Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

yashmobile.store ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it when you shop with us.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details — name, phone number, email address and delivery address you provide at checkout.
  • Payment information — processed securely by our payment partner Razorpay. We never store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • Usage data — basic, anonymous information such as pages visited, used only to improve the store.
  • Local browser storage — your cart and wishlist are saved in your own browser so they persist between visits. This data stays on your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, pack and deliver your order.
  • To send order confirmations, dispatch updates and support replies.
  • To prepare bulk / B2B quotations when you request one.
  • To comply with applicable Indian tax and legal requirements.

3. Sharing your information

We share only what is necessary with trusted partners — courier companies (for delivery), our payment gateway Razorpay (for secure payment), and communication providers (for SMS/email updates). We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use minimal browser storage to remember your cart and wishlist. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings; doing so will empty your cart and wishlist.

5. Data security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Checkout is handled by a PCI-DSS compliant payment partner. Access to order data is limited to staff who need it to fulfil your order.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by writing to us at care@yashmobile.store or calling +91 84510 66698 (11am–7pm, Mon–Sat).
